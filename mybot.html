Policies

Before you book

2

Active appointments max

You can have up to two online appointments booked at once, scheduled up to two weeks in advance.

15 min

Cancel or reschedule

Make changes at least 15 minutes before the start time. Canceling on time doesn't count as a no-show.

15 min

Join window

If you don't join within the first 15 minutes, the session is canceled and counted as a no-show.

5th

No-show locks your account

More than four no-shows locks your account. Email or call the lab during office hours to unlock it.

Tutors can help with

  • Understanding course concepts
  • Practice problems and ungraded homework
  • Studying for exams
  • Pre-lab and post-lab work for science lab courses

Tutors can't help with

  • Take-home exams
  • Any graded work
  • The actual lab experiments
